Notes on this copy:
- Obtained through the npm package "quran" (qzaidi), which bundles the Tanzil
  Uthmani text. That package prepended the Basmala to verse 1 of each surah
  (except 1 and 9); Noor removes that prefix, restoring Tanzil's original
  verse text. The Basmala is shown as a separate header in the app.
- Integrity: SHA-256 0edb6b0f46f7b4216a8f332cd141bed075879df723a2b1f6dcc5e6a34df071a5
  (checked at runtime; the reader shows an unavailable state on mismatch).
- Cross-checked against the King Fahd Complex Uthmani text (via QuranEnc /
  npm "quran-json"): the consonantal skeleton is identical for 6234 of 6236
  verses; 12:39 and 12:41 differ only in a known orthographic variant (يصاحبي).
